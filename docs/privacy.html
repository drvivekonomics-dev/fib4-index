Privacy policy

FIB-4 METAHEALTH360 · Last updated 25 August 2026

FIB-4 METAHEALTH360 is an on-device clinical calculator. It does not create an account and it does not send lab values to a server.

What is stored

  • Age, AST, ALT, platelets, protocol, optional initials, and the calculated score — only if you tap Save.
  • Your last-used protocol and platelet unit.
  • A one-time acknowledgement of the clinical disclaimer.

All of that stays in the app’s local storage on this phone.

What is not collected

  • No name, phone number, email, or medical-record identifier (unless you type initials into the optional label).
  • No analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs that upload data.
  • No cloud backup by the app.

Sharing

If you tap Share, the operating system share sheet sends only the text you chose to share. That is under your control.

Deleting data

Clear saved scores in Saved or More. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored by FIB-4 METAHEALTH360.

Medical use

Decision support for clinicians. Not a diagnostic device. Does not replace clinical judgement, elastography, or biopsy.
